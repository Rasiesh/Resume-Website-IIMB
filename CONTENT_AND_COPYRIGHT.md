# Content and copyright notes

## Writing style
The site uses short first-person sentences and keeps each project page focused on three things:
- what it was;
- what you worked on;
- what came from the experience.

The timeline shows only dates you have supplied. Undated chapters are shown without a date label.

## Copyright line
The footer uses:

`© 2026 Rasiesh Agarwal. All rights reserved.`

This is a standard copyright notice. It does not register a copyright or prove ownership of material created by someone else. Keep permission for the collaboration screenshots and any other third-party photographs. If a brand name, logo or trademark appears, it remains the property of its owner.

## Updating the year
When the year changes, search the HTML files for `© 2026` and replace it with the current year.
