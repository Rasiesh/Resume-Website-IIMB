About

Built by
experience.

My path has moved through different industries, teams and ideas. The common thread is building.

The beginning

I started freelancing in March 2022. Before that, I was exploring music, learning online and reaching out to people who were already building things.

Freelance work taught me to deal with clients, deadlines and the details behind a good delivery. I continued until June 2023, then stepped away from digital work and joined Sea & Shore, a maritime company built for seafarers, by seafarers.

Building across borders

In September 2024, I returned to agency work with Ageside.ai. Through April 2026, I worked on AI services, automation and business systems for projects connected to Moldova, Ireland and Greece. I also worked on a UAE engagement from November 2025 to January 2026, and a two-month project in the United States with a Mexican-led cosmetics brand.

Alongside this, I started CDC with my close friend Eric. It grew into a global community of more than 150 members, with workshops on personal growth and entrepreneurship.

Products and experiments

Hoptic’s took me into consumer technology through AI-enabled smart eyewear in the UAE. Later, I returned to India with my agency partners, completed an AI-supported operating system for a UAE laundry business and sold the agency, with associated project rights, to a UAE consultancy.

RoBee’s was my move into natural, handmade D2C products. We started with ₹10,000, reached an estimated valuation of ₹1 lakh in three months and took the brand to two Delhi-NCR pop-ups. It is now being rebranded.

I’m also developing FlowCom, an AI-enabled POS and business-management product, and starting a company in dental and oral care.

People and ideas

YouTube has been a steady source of learning, and my own channel is part of that journey. I also worked on WigglePop, a separate children’s content project. I’ve learned from people across markets and had the opportunity to receive guidance from Dr. Edward Rogers, Manoj Kohli and others.

In February 2024, I wrote about customer behaviour in a LinkedIn article: Are Your Customers Lying to You? ↗
