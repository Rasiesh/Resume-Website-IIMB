Product / FlowCom

FlowCom

A simpler way to run everyday business operations.

FlowCom is an AI-enabled point-of-sale and business-management product for small businesses.

One place for daily operations

It brings sales, customer records and business workflows into one system, helping teams see what was sold, to whom and what needs to happen next.

WhatsApp built in

Integrated WhatsApp messaging is designed to make routine updates and customer follow-ups easier to manage.

Why FlowCom

The idea came from problems I saw in client businesses: scattered records, manual follow-ups and disconnected tools. FlowCom aims to make those everyday tasks simpler.
