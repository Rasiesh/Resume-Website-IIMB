Case notes / HOPTIC’S

Hoptic’s
Built through experience.

A UAE-based consumer technology experiment exploring AI-enabled smart eyewear.

Chapter contents

The storyFull timelineAll projects

Hoptic’s was my move from selling services to thinking about a physical product — and the behaviour a new product category asks customers to adopt.

The product idea

The venture explored next-generation AI-enabled smart glasses. The ambition was to make technology more accessible in everyday moments, with eyewear as the interface. It was a consumer-tech experiment built and run in the UAE.

I approached the opportunity through product positioning, brand presentation and go-to-market thinking. Kickstarter and dropshipping were part of the commercial exploration, which meant thinking carefully about how to explain a product, build confidence and reach customers who had not yet formed a habit around it.

What the experience taught me

Technology alone does not create demand. Customers need to understand what changes in their lives, why the product is worth adopting and what trust signals exist before they buy. The work pushed me to connect product thinking with storytelling, distribution and customer expectations.

Hoptic’s remains an important chapter because it changed the kinds of questions I ask: not only “Can this be built?” but “Will someone want it, understand it and use it?”
