The idea

CDC began with a simple idea: make it easier for people to learn from one another and move forward together.

Eric and I started the community in September 2024. It grew to more than 150 members interested in personal growth and entrepreneurship.

What we built

We hosted workshops and developed an incubator-style space for people to share ideas, find support and take action.

Building CDC taught me that a strong community grows through trust and consistency.
